WHAT THE PROGRAMME COVERS

Personal coaching that turns goals into a workable routine.

Simon works with each participant to identify realistic next steps around:

  • Movement and exercise
  • Eating habits and nutrition awareness
  • Sleep and recovery
  • Consistency, confidence and personal accountability
