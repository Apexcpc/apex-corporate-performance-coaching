Clear answers for decision-makers FAQ

Workplace wellbeing,
made practical.

What does the programme include?

It can combine a workplace workshop with confidential one-to-one consultations and monthly coaching. Participants work on realistic routines for movement, eating habits, sleep and recovery, with follow-up to review progress and adapt next steps.

How does this benefit our organisation?

Employees receive support tailored to their goals and schedules, while managers gain a practical way to encourage healthier routines beyond a one-off wellbeing event. A pilot lets your organisation review participation and feedback before deciding whether to expand.

Can the programme work around a busy team?

Yes. Coaching is built around real working schedules and each participant’s starting point. The format and pilot scope are discussed with your organisation before the programme begins.

Where does Apex work with organisations?

Apex is available to organisations in Dubai and across the UAE, with UK and international enquiries also welcome. Contact Simon to discuss the right format for your team.

Is this medical or clinical care?

No. Apex provides general health education and lifestyle coaching, not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Participation is voluntary and confidential.

How much does a programme cost?

Programme options and fees are tailored to the organisation, team size and agreed scope. Start with a conversation to discuss a suitable pilot.
